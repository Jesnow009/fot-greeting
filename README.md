# 💖 Romantic Love Greeting Website

A mobile-first, interactive website crafted to greet your girlfriend and express your love in an unforgettable, lovely way.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **💌 3D Animated Sealed Envelope**:
   - Opens with a wax heart seal when she taps the screen.
   - Smooth 3D flap unsealing animation with letter slide-out and chime sounds.
2. **⏳ Real-Time Love Counter**:
   - Displays live days, hours, minutes, and seconds since your special day, ticking continuously with a beating heart animation.
3. **📜 Handwritten Love Letter**:
   - A warm, romantic message displayed in an authentic paper pin card with cursive handwriting.
4. **📸 Sweet Polaroid Memory Reel**:
   - Polaroid snapshots with washi tape, gentle tilts, captions, and tap heart effects.
   - Upload your own real couple photos directly in the customizer!
5. **🌸 "Reasons Why I Love You" 3D Flip Cards**:
   - Interactive pastel cards that flip over in 3D when tapped to reveal sweet reasons and compliments.
6. **🎟️ Exclusive Redeemable Love Coupons**:
   - Cute coupons ("Midnight Snack Run", "Pampering Massage", "Movie Night Dictator", "Win Any Argument").
   - She can tap "Redeem" to stamp them with a cute "REDEEMED!" seal.
7. **💓 "Send Me Infinite Love" Heart Shower**:
   - Tap-counter with floating particle hearts exploding upwards and sweet romantic quotes.
8. **💍 The Big Question & Runaway "No" Button**:
   - Playful "Will you be my forever?" proposal card.
   - If she tries to touch or hover the "No" button, it mischievously teleports away with cute tease messages!
   - Tapping "YES, FOREVER!" triggers full-screen confetti hearts and a celebration fanfare!
9. **🎵 Web Audio API Procedural Music**:
   - Built-in music box / lofi ambient melody synthesized in pure code (no missing audio files, works completely offline!).
   - Floating music toggle pill.
10. **⚙️ Built-in Customizer & Magic Shareable Link**:
    - Tap "⚙️ Customize" at top-left to adjust names, relationship date, and love message.
    - Click **"Copy Magic Link For Her Phone"** to generate a link that encodes your personalized details directly in the URL!

## 🚀 How to Open and Test Locally

1. Open File Explorer and navigate to:
   ```
   D:\Movies\love-greeting\
   ```
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in Google Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
3. Tap **"⚙️ Customize"** in the top-left corner to:
   - Set **Her Name** (e.g. your girlfriend's name or pet nickname).
   - Set **Your Name**.
   - Set your **Anniversary Date** or the day you first met.
   - Write your own custom love letter.
   - Add your photos.
   - Click **Save & Preview**.

## 📱 How to Send It to Her Phone (Free in 2 Minutes)

### Option A: Netlify Drop (Easiest - 1 Minute)
1. Go to [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the whole `love-greeting` folder onto the page.
3. Netlify will instantly give you a free public link (e.g., `https://lovely-greeting-xyz.netlify.app`).
4. Open the link on your browser, click **"⚙️ Customize"**, enter your details, click **"Copy Magic Link For Her Phone"**, and paste the link to her on WhatsApp, iMessage, or Instagram!

### Option B: GitHub Pages (Free & Permanent)
1. Upload the files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`) to a new GitHub repository.
2. In the repository settings, enable **GitHub Pages** on the `main` branch.
3. Share your GitHub Pages URL with her!
